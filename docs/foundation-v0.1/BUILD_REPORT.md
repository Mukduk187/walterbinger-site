# Living Map Foundation v0.1

Date: 2026-07-25

## What This Draft Establishes

- The approved professional page remains the public front door.
- Its constellation mark opens the relative living map.
- PREP / PERP Field Tools is available from the professional page and as a
  first-class world in the map.
- Stars share one durable data model for position, depth, lens weights,
  relationships, artifacts, resources, and routes.
- Lens selections apply deterministic force fields. They do not randomly
  reshuffle the sky.
- Related authored worlds use curved, weighted paths.
- Single click pins a world. Double click enters it. Pinned artifacts orbit
  continuously around their source.
- All eight lenses converge through Gratitude into the Snow Globe.
- Snow Globe memories reopen their worlds without retriggering convergence.
