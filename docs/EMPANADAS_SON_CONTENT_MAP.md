# Empanadas, Son! Content Map

Date: 2026-07-19

This note preserves the content grammar to design later. It does not authorize explanatory panels on the current sky.

## Exhibit layers

- **Star artwork:** Walter's original menu empanada remains the navigational object.
- **Orbiting photographs:** a small curated set from the historical official archive shows place, process, people, and finished food.
- **Short text artifacts:** dates, addresses, menu names, or one-line context can appear when an image needs grounding.
- **Document artifacts:** menus, press features, and review pages can behave like found papers rather than generic cards.
- **External evidence:** reviews and listings should open their original sources and remain visibly distinct from Walter-authored material.
- **Long-form story:** the full retrospective belongs deeper inside the Empanadas, Son! world, not in the landing sky.

## Link behavior to prototype later

- A photograph should remain a photograph; a source link should not make the whole image feel like an ad.
- A short line of text can sit on an orbital path or appear as a restrained caption after focus.
- Reviews should be discoverable as evidence, not summarized into sales copy.
- External links need a small, consistent visual cue and should open without disrupting the visitor's position in the sky.
- The content model must support positive, mixed, and contextual sources. Transparency is part of the archive, not a slogan printed over it.
