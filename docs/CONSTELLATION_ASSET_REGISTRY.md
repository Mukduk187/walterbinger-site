# Constellation Asset Registry v1

Date: 2026-07-19

Purpose: keep symbols, tattoos, stars, worlds, and reference images from collapsing into one another. A reference image is not automatically a destination. A personal artifact is not automatically an umbrella symbol. Every asset needs a job before it enters the site.

## Classification Rules

Content source:

- Material from Walter's actual life, work, writing, art, or archive.
- Can become a star, world, artifact, proof object, or destination.
- Examples: Empanadas, Son! menu art, the casting hand photo, Nimbus images, Fire in Balance photos, essays, project documents.

Symbolic source:

- A real emblem, civic mark, cultural symbol, object, or visual structure that directly represents a cosmology node.
- Can inform the compact tattoo/star mark.
- Examples: Brooklyn Bridge, Dirigo pine and star, Sun of May.

Structural reference:

- A reference for composition, motion, line quality, interaction, or scene behavior.
- Does not become content unless Walter explicitly promotes it.
- Examples: observatory linework, star-map interfaces, zoom-art transitions, field-guide sheets.

## Interaction Rules From Latest Feedback

- Small interactive star icons should be roughly 50 percent larger than the earlier dormant size.
- When color is active, clickable/discoverable stars should glow or twinkle enough to distinguish them from passive graphite sky marks.
- Passive stars can fill the field, but they stay graphite, unlabeled, and non-clickable until a lens makes them relevant.
- One lens behaves like gravity.
- Two lenses may behave like a blended orbit.
- Three or more lenses reorganize by relationship strength, not an automatic circle.
- Every active frequency contributes its stored composition weight to an object's central pull; combined frequency attraction sets radius and pairwise relationship sets neighborhood.
- Objects should slide into position and leave a brief trail.
- Hover must not reset or hiccup existing animations.
- Mobile needs a taller, scrollable universe map rather than a squeezed desktop map.
