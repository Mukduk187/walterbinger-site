# Walter Directives

Date: 2026-07-19

This file is the current authority layer for WalterBinger.com.

Use Walter's direct commands and design guidance as binding. Treat Scout/ChatGPT commentary as optional interpretation only. Do not implement Scout's language, metaphors, or structure unless Walter has restated, selected, or clearly approved that specific idea.

## Authority Order

1. Walter's direct messages in the current conversation.
2. Walter-authored guidance inside pasted attachments.
3. Concrete constraints already accepted into the repo notes.
4. Scout/ChatGPT commentary, only as background signal.

When these conflict, follow Walter.

## Direct Design Commands

The site should not feel sterile, corporate, or like a professional persona assembled for approval.

It should feel mostly Walter. Assistant contribution should be restrained.

Ask pointed questions before building large surfaces. Do not generate a whole website from a vague vibe and then force a rebuild.

Do research and use the material, but do not use all of it. Authenticity and integrity come through transparency and curation, not dumping the archive.

Do not take artifacts literally. A table, casting, photo, or Pinterest reference is source language unless Walter explicitly says to use it as the visible object.

Professionalism is the container, not the sensation.

The page should feel authentic, perceptive, kind, creative, communicative, and professionally held.

Authentic does not mean messy or careless. The work should feel rough and deliberate, not unfinished by accident.

Avoid cringe, incense, patchouli, fake-hippie, fake-mystical, fake-Rick, CIA-narc-bot energy.

Do not make the site a cathedral to Walter's ego. It should build outward credibility and inward stability, accountability, self-ownership, and control.

The site should be part blog, part working portfolio, part revenue builder, and part recruiting magnet.

The site itself should be an artifact of thinking: proof that makes someone want to engage and make new proof with Walter.

It should allow different visitors to project their own doorway into the work. Walter is dynamic; the site should not flatten him into one label.

Motion should emerge as people progress. No motion-sickness.

The experience can use orientation shifts, 4D/multiverse feeling, Jamiroquai-style playful spatial movement, and small easter eggs, but those references should not dominate the interface.

The landing page should feel like Walter is custom drawing the visitor his business card.

Latest homepage mechanic from Walter:

> It should almost look like just stars, but then when you hover over one, it turns into a flash tattoo choice board, connected with a constellation. Each star in the set expands to show the image hiding beneath it. Expanding one expands the others connected to it, depending on which lens you have selected, for instance Location + Time, or Maine and Argentina.

## Current Homepage Requirements

At rest:

- mostly warm white or paper field
- sparse stars or dots
- little to no explanatory homepage copy
- not a conventional hero section
- not a card grid
- not a personal-brand explainer
- no visible fully exposed illustrated landscape

On interaction:

- hovering/focusing a star lights it up
- the star expands into the hidden image beneath it
- the hidden image should feel like a simple flash-tattoo drawing
- related stars expand or wake according to the selected lens
- magenta can connect, circle, underline, correct, or draw paths
- constellations show relationships, not decorative wiring
- labels and explanation come after discovery, not before

Lenses:

- the same stars/drawings can connect differently under different lenses
- examples Walter gave: `Location + Time`, `Maine`, `Argentina`
- lensing should feel like a way of looking, not a database filter
- unselected stars stay present but dormant
- a selected lens should also bend the field into a center-out spatial reading
- one lens creates a gravitational emphasis
- two lenses may create a blended orbit if the relationship is visually legible
- three or more lenses must not default to an arbitrary circle
- with three or more lenses, position should be relationship-weighted: proximity and distance tell the viewer how strongly objects matter to each other
- every object has an explicit attraction weight for every frequency; the combined weights of the active frequencies determine its radius from the current center of attraction
- composition determines how far inward an object is pulled, while pairwise relationship determines which objects settle near one another
- the lens controls are not stars or destinations themselves; they change color, proximity, and the constellations that become visible
- exploring a star changes the observer's origin, so the zodiac must recompute around that new point of view while preserving the active lenses
- lens reorientation should drift calmly and leave a brief visible trail
- hover and focus changes must not rebuild or restart running orbit, twinkle, dash, or satellite animations
- transportation between stars remains the distinct zoom-travel motion

Discoverable stars:

- small interactive objects should be approximately 50% larger than the previous dormant size
- when a lens is active, related objects should emit a restrained, staggered colored twinkle
- the black drawing remains steady while the spectral outline supplies the glow
- this visual distinction allows the field to gain many ordinary background stars without obscuring which objects can be discovered
- passive guide stars remain graphite, still, non-clickable, and unlabeled
- passive marks can become drawing anchors without pretending that every point is a destination
- the landing zodiac may hold an empty observational center; after Explore, the selected world becomes the visible origin
- with multiple lenses, stronger shared relationships occupy inner orbital positions and partial relationships sit farther out

## Symbol And Tattoo Corrections

The star symbols are not generic icons. They are compact marks that can expand into meaning.

Current symbol guidance:

- Maine should use a windswept Dirigo pine with an off-center star, likely red. It must not read as a Christmas tree.
- Empanadas, Son! should use the original store sun graphic with menu empanadas orbiting like moons or planetary rings.
- Food is a larger umbrella world and needs its own symbol distinct from Empanadas, Son!
- Thinking in 4D should use a torus or toroidal field as the primary compact symbol.
- A tesseract or hypercube can be used as article/environment art for Thinking in 4D, with the Interstellar black-hole/tesseract sequence as a reference.
- The Art hand is currently approved as a useful treatment reference: clean interior, animated color confined to the outline, and multiple readable satellites.
- Brooklyn and Argentina remain plausible flagship symbols, but should be refined from direct civic or cultural source material rather than generic generated marks.

Observatory correction:

- Observatory is not currently a major star, world, or destination.
- The observatory image was a visual/style reference only.
- Retain its cartoony architectural linework, charming scale, grounded building composition, and celestial atmosphere as art-direction guidance.
- Do not add telescope/dome navigation as a flagship concept unless Walter later creates or approves content that belongs there.

Mobile universe:

- do not squeeze the wide desktop sky into a shallow mobile strip
- mobile should use a taller coordinate projection and deliberate vertical scrolling
- preserve the symbols' proportions while spreading their positions from top to bottom
- the name and lens controls may scroll away so the visitor can enter a full-screen field
- expanded mobile objects should shift toward a usable center so their satellites and Explore control remain onscreen

## Curated Art Rule

- Walter's own art, photographs, graphics, handwriting, menus, diagrams, and objects are the preferred direct source material.
- Public-domain historical art can provide structural scaffolding when its source is recorded.
- Outside artists and traditions are aesthetic references, not material to copy.
- New navigational drawings should be based on something real and direct, or developed collaboratively with Walter.
- Do not fill gaps with arbitrary generated illustrations that merely imitate the desired mood.

## Working Rule
