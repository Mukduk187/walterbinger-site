# Draft 23: Fluid Relations

- One lens keeps the gravitational center-out reading.
- Exactly two lenses keep the approved blended orbit.
- Three or more lenses use a deterministic relationship layout: shared composition and explicit links pull objects closer, while weak relationships create distance.
- The strongest relationships receive faint curved routes rather than an automatic closed circle.
- Lens changes slide objects into place for 1.08 seconds and leave a short fluorescent ink trail.
- Hover and focus now update attention in place; they do not recreate satellite previews or restart their animation clocks.
- Expanded satellite systems persist across lens reorientation and only update their screen position.
